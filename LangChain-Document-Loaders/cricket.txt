CRICKET - QUICK OVERVIEW

Cricket is a bat-and-ball game played between two teams of 11 players each.

BASIC RULES:
- One team bats while the other bowls and fields.
- The batting team tries to score runs by hitting the ball and running between two sets of wooden stumps (wickets), or by hitting boundaries (4 runs) or sixes (6 runs).
- The bowling team tries to get batsmen "out" (via bowled, caught, LBW, run out, stumped, etc.) and restrict runs.
- An "over" consists of 6 balls bowled by one bowler.
- Once a team is all out (10 wickets down) or overs are completed, the innings ends and teams swap roles.

FORMATS:
- Test Cricket: Played over 5 days, most traditional format.
- One Day International (ODI): Limited to 50 overs per side.
- T20: Limited to 20 overs per side, fast-paced format.
- T10 / The Hundred: Newer, shorter formats.

KEY TERMS:
- Wicket: The three stumps + bails, or the act of dismissing a batsman.
- Over: A set of 6 legal deliveries bowled.
- Boundary: Ball reaches the edge of the field (4 runs if it bounces, 6 if it clears on the full).
- LBW: Leg Before Wicket - a common way of getting out.
- Century: A batsman scoring 100 or more runs in an innings.
- Duck: A batsman dismissed for 0 runs.

MAJOR TOURNAMENTS:
- ICC Cricket World Cup (ODI format)
- ICC T20 World Cup
- Indian Premier League (IPL) - major T20 league
- The Ashes - historic Test series between England and Australia
